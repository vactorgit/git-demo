**0:30** One. 1963. X-Men number 1, written by Stan Lee and drawn by Jack Kirby. It introduced the original five X-Men: Cyclops, Marvel Girl, Beast, Iceman and Angel. It also introduced Professor X and his enemy, Magneto.

**1:05** Quick pause. If you want to start reading right now, I made a free X-Men Starter Kit: three comics to start with, sent to your email. The link is in the description.

**1:25** Two. 1975 to 1981. Giant-Size X-Men number 1 by Len Wein and Dave Cockrum brought the X-Men back after five years with a new team. Chris Claremont took over the series soon after and wrote it for years. In 1980, Claremont and John Byrne told the Dark Phoenix Saga in Uncanny X-Men numbers 129 to 138. Jean Grey becomes the Dark Phoenix, and the story ends with her sacrifice. In 1981 came Days of Future Past, in numbers 141 and 142, the story the 2014 movie is built on.

**2:35** Three. 1986 to 1996. Mutant Massacre in 1986 was the first big X-Men crossover. The Marauders attack the Morlocks, a community of mutants living underground. In 1991, X-Men number 1, by Chris Claremont and Jim Lee, sold more than 8 million copies, the best-selling comic ever. In 1995, Age of Apocalypse threw the X-Men into an alternate reality ruled by Apocalypse. In 1996, Onslaught pulled in nearly every Marvel hero.

**4:00** Four. 2001 to 2005. Grant Morrison and Frank Quitely's New X-Men ran from 2001 to 2004. It opens with Cassandra Nova using Sentinels to destroy the mutant nation of Genosha. Then in 2005, House of M, by Brian Michael Bendis and Olivier Coipel, ends with Scarlet Witch saying "No more mutants." Most mutants lose their powers. Fans call it M-Day.

**5:20** Five. 2011 and 2012. In Schism, by Jason Aaron and Carlos Pacheco, Cyclops and Wolverine split the X-Men into two teams. A year later, in Avengers vs. X-Men, the Phoenix Force splits and possesses five X-Men. The fight turns the Avengers and the X-Men against each other.

**6:25** Six. 2019 to 2024. In House of X and Powers of X, Jonathan Hickman, Pepe Larraz and R.B. Silva built Krakoa, a nation for mutants, and relaunched the whole line. Then Fall of X ended it, with the last issue in June 2024. In July 2024, X-Men From the Ashes relaunched the books with X-Men, Uncanny X-Men and Exceptional X-Men.
