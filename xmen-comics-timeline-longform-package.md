**1:00** Then something strange happened. By 1970, Marvel had stopped making new X-Men stories. For about five years, the book only reprinted old issues.

**1:25** Quick pause. If you want to start reading right now, I made a free X-Men Starter Kit: three comics to start with, sent to your email. The link is in the description.

**1:45** Two. 1975 to 1981. Giant-Size X-Men number 1, by Len Wein and Dave Cockrum, brought the X-Men back with a brand-new team. It was the first new X-Men story in five years. Chris Claremont took over the book with number 94 and wrote it for years. In 1980, Claremont and John Byrne told the Dark Phoenix Saga, starting in Uncanny X-Men number 129. Jean Grey becomes the Dark Phoenix. Then in 1981 came Days of Future Past, in numbers 141 and 142, the story the 2014 movie is built on. Key issues: Giant-Size X-Men number 1, Uncanny X-Men number 129, and numbers 141 and 142.

**3:00** Three. 1986 to 1996. In 1986, in Mutant Massacre, a group called the Marauders attacked the Morlocks, a community of mutants living underground. It was such a hit that Marvel started holding a big X-Men crossover every year. In 1991, X-Men number 1, by Chris Claremont and Jim Lee, sold about 8.1 million copies. That's the Guinness record for the best-selling single comic. In 1995, Age of Apocalypse replaced the X-Men books with a world where Apocalypse conquered North America. In 1996, Onslaught, a villain created from the minds of Professor X and Magneto, pushed the Avengers and the X-Men to team up. Key reads: X-Men number 1 from 1991, and Age of Apocalypse.

**4:30** Four. 2001 to 2005. Grant Morrison and Frank Quitely's New X-Men started in 2001. It opens with a villain named Cassandra Nova using Sentinels to destroy the mutant nation of Genosha. Then in 2005, House of M, by Brian Michael Bendis and Olivier Coipel, ends with Scarlet Witch saying "No more mutants." Most mutants lose their powers. Key reads: New X-Men number 114, and House of M.

**5:50** Five. 2011 and 2012. In Schism, written by Jason Aaron, Cyclops and Wolverine split the X-Men into two teams. A year later, in Avengers vs. X-Men, the Phoenix Force splits into five pieces and possesses Cyclops, Emma Frost, Namor, Colossus and Magik. Cyclops kills Professor X. Key reads: Schism, and Avengers vs. X-Men.

**7:00** Six. 2019 to 2024. In House of X and Powers of X, Jonathan Hickman, Pepe Larraz and R.B. Silva built Krakoa, a mutant nation, and relaunched the whole line. It ended with Fall of X, and the last issue came out in June 2024. Then X-Men From the Ashes relaunched the books. Key reads: House of X number 1, and Fall of the House of X.

**8:00** So what's the pattern? [Your thesis in your words: what ties these six eras together. Say "this is my read."]
