**0:00** Doctor Doom was warned. One person told Victor von Doom his machine had a mistake in it, and he threw that person out. The machine exploded, and Doom has blamed the man who warned him ever since. That's his origin in the comics, and it's the key to what Robert Downey Jr. is walking into in Avengers: Doomsday.

**0:13** [Your receipt: what you have read about Doom, and when. One sentence.]

**0:19** Start with the boy. Victor von Doom is born in Latveria to a Romani family. His father, Werner, is a healer. His mother, Cynthia, is a witch, and she dies young. When a ruler's wife dies in Werner's care, the ruler's men come for him. Werner and young Victor run into the mountains. In the snow, Werner wraps his coat around his son. Victor lives. Werner dies of exposure.

**0:36** Victor digs up a chest that belonged to his mother. It's full of potions and books, and that's where his magic starts. In later comics, her soul is trapped in hell. He grows into an inventor and sorcerer who protects his people and tricks the wealthy, and when the ruler's men catch him, they only catch robot copies.

**0:50** Quick pause. If you've been meaning to start reading comics, I made a free Starter Kit: three comics to start with, sent to your email. The link is in the description.

**0:57** The mask came before the man. Doctor Doom debuts in Fantastic Four number 5, in July 1962, by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby. He kidnaps Sue Richards, and he sends the rest of the team back in time, with his time machine, to steal Blackbeard's treasure chest. When Doom speaks, Reed recognizes his voice. It's a college classmate, a brilliant student who blew up a lab and his own face. Reed tricks him by swapping the treasure for old chains, and Ben Grimm ends up as the pirate history remembers as Blackbeard. Doom's full origin doesn't come until two years later, in Fantastic Four Annual number 2.

**1:22** A scholarship brings Victor to a university in America, and that's where he meets Reed Richards. Reed tries to be friendly. He even offers to be roommates. Victor insists on a private room, so Reed ends up rooming with Ben Grimm. And Victor takes it personally.

**1:33** Now Victor builds a machine. He wants to reach the netherworld, so he can reach his mother. Reed sees his notes, finds mistakes in the calculations, and tells him. Victor doesn't even look at what Reed wrote. He throws him out. And he turns the machine on.

**1:45** Reed was right. The machine explodes in Victor's face. It scars him, and the university expels him. And Victor decides it was Reed's fault. In the comics, he believes Reed sabotaged his work.

**1:52** [Beat 2, in your words: a time someone tried to warn you, or a time you wouldn't listen. One or two sentences. This is the heart of the video. I don't write this one.]

**2:06** So Victor goes to Tibet, where monks forge him a suit of armor and an iron mask. And he can't wait. He puts the mask on before the metal has cooled. Then he returns to Latveria, overthrows the monarchy, and rules it.

**2:16** Today, Doom is a scientific genius and a sorcerer. His armor gives him superhuman strength and weapons. And he rules Latveria with diplomatic immunity. His origin also places him in a Romani community, and one Slashfilm writer points out that the movies have never acknowledged that. [Your read on that, in your words. Say "this is my read."]

**2:33** So how bad is the scar? In some stories it's a small scar, and Victor's pride makes it the end of the world. In others the burning mask does the real damage. Different writers answer it differently, and the comics never fully settle it. [Your read: what do you think the scar is really about? Say "this is my read."]

**2:50** And he never lets go of his mother. In Astonishing Tales number 8, Doom fights a demon once a year to free her soul. Later comics name that demon Mephisto. Then, in Triumph and Torment, a 1989 story by Roger Stern and Mike Mignola, Doctor Strange wins a sorcery contest, with Doom in second place. As the winner, Strange owes Doom a favor. Doom asks for help saving his mother's soul. The two of them go down to hell. [Only if you've read it: your one sentence on it.]

**3:15** Back in that same origin issue, Doom is still sure he's right. He invites the Fantastic Four to a party at the Latverian embassy, and he drugs their drinks. Everyone starts seeing the others attack them, and the team turns on itself. Then they work out what's happening. A contest follows to see whose mind is better, each with a device that runs on thought. Reed offers Doom one last drink. It's the same drug. Doom believes he has destroyed Reed, and he walks away in triumph. He was fooled by his own trick. That's the root of the feud. Doom blames Reed for his scar, he wants to prove he's the better mind, and he never admits that Reed is a genius too.

**3:44** A few years later, in Fantastic Four numbers 57 to 60, Doom invites the Silver Surfer to his castle and drains the Surfer's cosmic power into himself. For a short time, he's almost all-powerful. He loses it, and the Surfer's board returns to its master.

**3:55** In 1981, in a story called Doomquest, Iron Man and Doom are thrown back in time to Camelot. Doom goes looking for the sorceress Morgan le Fay, because he thinks she can save his mother. Her price is that Doom helps her destroy King Arthur. The writer, David Michelinie, later called Doom "Tony's reflection in a dark mirror."

**4:09** But Doom isn't only a man who won't listen. In the comics, he takes in an orphaned boy named Kristoff and raises him as his heir. The boy ends up believing he is Doom. So is Doom just a monster? The comics never let it be that simple.

**4:20** Then there's Reed's daughter. When Sue Richards's pregnancy turns dangerous, Doom helps deliver the baby. His price is the right to name her, and he calls her Valeria, after his long-lost first love. He becomes her godfather. And then, with the newborn in his arms, he casts a spell that makes her his familiar, a link between Doom and Reed's family.

**4:35** He uses that link in Unthinkable, by Mark Waid and Mike Wieringo. Doom attacks the Fantastic Four through their children. He sends Reed's son, Franklin, to hell. Reed has always refused to accept magic, and now the only way to save his family is to learn it. Doctor Strange teaches him the basics. Reed frees his family, and Doom ends up in hell in Franklin's place. That's how far Doom's pride goes. He gave up science for magic, and he used a baby to get at the man who warned him.

**4:56** In 2010, in Doomwar, Doom invades Wakanda and plunders its vibranium. It takes the X-Men, the Fantastic Four and two Black Panthers to stand against him.

**5:03** Doom has chased godlike power before. In the original Secret Wars, in 1984, he steals the Beyonder's godlike power, and it's too vast for him to control. The Beyonder takes it back. In 2015, in Secret Wars by Jonathan Hickman and Esad Ribić, he tries again. The universes are colliding and the multiverse is being destroyed. Doom, Doctor Strange and the Molecule Man confront the beings behind it, the Beyonders, and this time Doom keeps their power. He builds a patchwork planet called Battleworld out of pieces of the lost worlds, and he rules it as God Emperor Doom.

**5:26** Doctor Strange is his closest ally there. In the end, Doom kills him, holds a funeral, and covers up what he did. And Reed points out the first thing Doom did with the power of a god. He took Reed's wife and children for himself.

**5:37** Then Reed comes for him. The Molecule Man cuts off most of Doom's power, so the fight is fair. Reed tells Doom he has never thought he was better than him, and he calls out his insecurity. Then the Molecule Man hands the Beyonders' power to Reed instead, and Battleworld ends. Reed uses it to rebuild the multiverse, one world at a time. And he heals the scars on Doom's face. In Latveria, Doom takes off his mask, finds his face whole, and laughs with joy. Doom was warned, and the man he blamed for everything is the one who gave him his face back.

**6:02** Afterward, Doom tries to be a hero. In Infamous Iron Man, by Brian Michael Bendis and Alex Maleev, he takes up the Iron Man name. And Marvel's biggest comic event of 2025 was called One World Under Doom, after he became Sorcerer Supreme.
