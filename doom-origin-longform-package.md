## Story check
- **One sentence:** The man who became Doctor Doom was warned that his machine had a mistake in it, threw out the person who warned him, and became Doom because of what happened next.
- **Through-line:** Victor wants to save his mother's soul. His pride won't let him hear that he might be wrong. The machine explodes, and the rest of his life is spent proving he was never wrong, by blaming Reed Richards.
- **Emotion map:** curiosity (warned?), sympathy (a boy who loses both parents), awe (a genius with a scholarship), dread (the warning ignored), grief (the explosion), pride and rage (the mask goes on hot), then a question (how will the movies tell it?).
- **What the viewer feels at the end:** that Doom is a tragedy before he is a villain, and that this makes him more dangerous.
- **Weak spots:** the long stretch from 1:55 to 4:15 is backstory. Keep each beat short and end each on a want or a loss. The 3:30 and 5:10 beats are yours.
