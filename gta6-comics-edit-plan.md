## Edit style (what makes it exciting)
- Pace: first beat shortest, each later beat a little longer. A visual change at least every 2-3 s. No dead air except the deliberate beats marked SILENCE.
- Look: neon pink and teal grade on you and on graphics. Sin City slot goes black and white, then the neon returns for the couple. Inspired by Vice City's mood, no Rockstar art, logo, font or sounds.
- Sound: original or licensed music only. Low tension drone in the cold open, driving beat from Slot 2, sly swing for Slot 3, siren-like rise at the midpoint, quiet for Violent Love, resolve at the end. Duck music under your voice about 12 dB.
- Graphic system: a CREW x/6 counter bottom-right from the intro on (six dots filling). A "POWERS: NONE" stamp each time the script says "No powers". A buy card (title, issues, who it is for) held 4 s after each slot. Use the same card layout six times so it reads as a system.
- No yellow text. Captions burned in, key word colored teal or pink.
- TV viewers cannot click: put a QR code and a short address on the Starter Kit card.
