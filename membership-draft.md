# YouTube Membership draft for This Comic COOKS! (saved 10 Nov 2026, launch no earlier than January 2027)

Status: DRAFT ONLY. Nothing is set up in YouTube Studio. Check Studio for eligibility, available prices and YouTube's revenue share before launching; I did not verify them.

## Launch conditions (all should be true first)
1. The email list is growing and you have sent at least three weekly emails.
2. Viewers have asked how to support you (comments, replies).
3. You have banked at least three months of the monthly reading note, if you include it.
4. You have decided what happens to Patreon (one paid member today): move it, or close it. Do not run both for long.
5. Your December schedule is known, so you only promise what you can keep up.

## The offer: one tier
- **Tier name:** The Kitchen (members are Line Cooks). Alternates: Back of House, The Cooks' Table.
- **Price:** $3.99 a month (a starting point; confirm in Studio).
- **Loyalty badges (4):** Prep Cook (new), Line Cook (2 months), Sous Chef (6 months), Head Chef (1 year).
- **Custom emojis (4 to 5):** a chef hat, a mutant X, a speech bubble that says "COOKS!", a comic panel, and a text emoji for "if you're nasty". (PNG, square, transparent background.)

## Perks (only these, so you can keep every promise)
1. **Early access:** each long-form goes to members 24 to 48 hours before everyone else. Zero extra work.
2. **A vote on the next video:** two or three topics from your requests list go to a members poll. You make the one that wins, if it passes your fact check.
3. **Your name on the credits slide** of every long-form (opt-in).
4. **Optional, only after three months are banked:** a monthly "What I'm Reading" note with Buy, Library or Skip verdicts, from books you have actually read.

Not included on purpose: weekly exclusives, live streams, personal reading lists, collector perks (pull lists, slabs). Add one only if the first month shows you have the time.

## Membership page text (draft, in your voice; edit freely)
> This Comic COOKS! is for people who want to love comics but don't know where to start. The free stuff stays free: every video, and the Starter Kit (3 comics to start with).
>
> If you want to back the channel, The Kitchen gets you my long-forms a day or two early, a vote on what I make next, and your name on the credits.
>
> What your support does not do: it never changes a verdict. If a book is a Skip, I'll say Skip. If a company sends me something free, I'll tell you.
>
> Thanks for being here. [Name], if you're nasty.

## Trust rules for members
- Never gate corrections or safety-of-trust items: a mistake gets a public pinned correction, not a members-only one.
- Disclose affiliate links and sponsors as usual, on screen and in the description.
- Answer members' comments like everyone else's, no faster and no slower.

## Launch checklist (when conditions are met)
1. Confirm eligibility, price options and the revenue share in YouTube Studio.
2. Design the four badges and the 4 to 5 emojis.
3. Paste the page text above and the perks list.
4. Turn on members-only early access for the next long-form.
5. Announce once in a long-form and once in the weekly email. No hard pitch before then.
6. Decide what to do with Patreon the same week.
