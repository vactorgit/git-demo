Prepared 5 Nov 2026 for Vactor (This Comic COOKS!). Edited video with graphics, about 8 to 9 minutes. Fill every `[ ]` blank in your own words. Idea from a viewer comment by @Ptdinoreaper ("a complete X-Men timeline explained shortly"). Publish by about 20 Nov.

This keeps the timeline the viewer asked for, but follows one character through it, so it is a story and not a list. v2 (`xmen-comics-timeline-longform-package.md`) stays as the era-by-era backup.

## Story check
- **One sentence:** Professor X's first student, the loyal leader, ends up being the one who kills him. Here is every step that got him there.
- **Through-line:** Cyclops wants to protect his people and be the leader Xavier trusted. Again and again he picks duty over his own heart. The obstacle is that he can't even open his eyes without a visor. The change is a boy who obeys Xavier becomes the man who kills him, and then has to live as a leader anyway.
- **Emotion map:**
  1. Warmth and trust (1963, Xavier picks him to lead)
  2. Unease (a boy who can't control his own power)
  3. Grief (Jean dies, Scott walks away)
  4. Guilt (he leaves his wife and baby when Jean turns up alive)
  5. Tension (Schism, the team splits)
  6. Shock (he kills Xavier)
  7. Bittersweet release (he dies, comes back as "Captain Krakoa," and returns in Doomsday)
- **What the viewer feels at the end:** that the villain of the X-Men's saddest story was a good man doing what he always did.
- **Weak spot:** Madelyne, Jean and Emma are a long soap-opera stretch. Keep each to one sentence. Cut anything that does not push Scott toward the killing.
