## Story check
- **One sentence:** The orphan Xavier took in becomes the leader who loses everything, and in the end he is the one who kills Xavier.
- **Through-line:** Cyclops wants to protect his people and be the leader Xavier trusted. Each loss pushes him to pick the mission over his own heart.
- **Emotion map:** shock and curiosity (the hook), sympathy (the boy and the orphanage), warmth (Xavier trusts him), ache (Jean, Madelyne), pressure (Storm, M-Day, Hope), dread (Phoenix), shock (Xavier dies), grief, then bittersweet release (he carries Xavier's dream, Krakoa, Doomsday).
- **What the viewer feels at the end:** that the saddest story in the X-Men was a good man doing what he always did.
- **Mirrors the opening:** a boy hurt by adults who used him, and later a man who hurts the one adult who didn't.
