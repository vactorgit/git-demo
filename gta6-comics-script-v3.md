GTA 6 is out today. Before you load in, let me show you a heist that goes wrong in a comic from twenty years ago.

[B-ROLL: Criminal: Coward panels, slow push-in. No music yet.]

Leo Patterson is a thief with rules. No guns. And he's famous for one thing: when a job goes bad, he runs.

Then someone from his past pitches him a score. Diamonds, locked inside police evidence. And a crooked cop is in on it.

This job is about to test every rule he has.

The last pick has a couple that the first pages tell you won't end well. You still won't look away.

### Slot 1: The Planner, Criminal

[CREW 1/6]

Our planner is Leo, from Criminal, by writer Ed Brubaker and artist Sean Phillips. It started in October 2006.

Leo grew up in a crew of thieves. His father led it, and that crew taught him the trade. Now Leo plans heists, and the underworld calls him a coward, because when a job goes bad, Leo runs.

No powers. His one skill is knowing when to leave.

So when that score comes knocking, his first answer is no. The book is about what happens next.

[CARD: Criminal Vol. 1: Coward, issues #1 to 5. Mature readers.]

[YOUR VERDICT, only if you read it: Buy / Library / Skip, one reason.]

If you've never read a comic, I put three to start with in a free Starter Kit. The link is below.

GTA gives you a respawn. Leo has to live with every mistake.

Every crew needs a planner. But a planner doesn't do the stealing. For that, you need a thief.

### Slot 2: The Thief, Black Cat

[CREW 2/6]

You know her from Spider-Man. Felicia Hardy. The Black Cat. She got her first ongoing series in 2019, from writer Jed MacKay and artist Travel Foreman.

Powers: none you need to worry about. She's a thief and an acrobat.

Issue one, she and her crew steal a prized painting. Clean job. One problem. She never cleared it with the Thieves Guild of New York, and the Guild doesn't forgive.

If you've played GTA for more than an hour, you know that feeling. You did the work. Someone bigger says you owe them.

So now Felicia is on the run from the Guild and its boss. That's the most GTA setup I've ever seen in a Marvel comic.

[CARD: Black Cat Vol. 1: Grand Theft Marvel, issues #1 to 5, MacKay and Foreman.]

[YOUR VERDICT if you read it.]

A planner and a thief. But nobody escapes on foot. Next up is the driver, and his superpower is the most GTA thing I've seen in a comic.

### Slot 3: The Driver, Superior Foes of Spider-Man

[CREW 3/6]

Meet Overdrive. James Beverley was a race car driver who wanted to be a superhero. It didn't work out.

What he got instead was nanites that transform any vehicle he touches. He can upgrade a car into whatever he wants. Walk away too long, and it changes back.

He's a walking mod shop. And he's barely a villain. He admits he only went bad for the reputation, and he plans to switch sides later, the way Hawkeye did.

His best book is The Superior Foes of Spider-Man, from 2013, by Nick Spencer and Steve Lieber. And he comes with a crew.

They call themselves the Sinister Six. There are five of them. They know.

Boomerang leads, and he fights with boomerangs. The Shocker has vibration gauntlets. The Beetle's armor gives her strength, durability and wings.

Their big score involves a painting of Doctor Doom.

And the book is running a con on you. It looks like a team book. It's really Boomerang telling you his version of events, and nothing about it is honest.

That's every GTA side character you've ever loved. Big talk. Small time. Zero loyalty.

[CARD: The Superior Foes of Spider-Man, 17 issues.]

### Midpoint re-hook

[CREW 3/6]

Halfway. Planner, thief, driver. That crew can pull a job.

But in GTA, the job is never the hard part. The hard part is after, when the whole city comes for you.

Slot four is the heat. And this comic turned it into an app.

### Slot 4: The Heat, Crowded

[CREW 4/6]

In Crowded, the world runs on apps. One of them is called Reapr. It lets anybody crowdfund an assassination.

Charlie Ellison lives in Los Angeles. Then someone puts a seven-figure bounty on her. She has thirty days to survive it.

That's a wanted level you can't hide from.

She can't afford real protection, so she hires a bodyguard off an app called Dfend. Her name is Vita. She's a low-rated bodyguard.

No powers. A target, a bargain-bin bodyguard, and a city full of strangers who want the payout.

It's by Christopher Sebela, with art by Ro Stein and Ted Brandt. Image Comics, 2018.

[CARD: Crowded Vol. 1.]

[YOUR VERDICT if you read it.]

And look at what Crowded is underneath. Two people. On the run. Stuck with each other. Hold onto that. It's where this whole crew is heading.

### Slot 5: The Map, Sin City

[CREW 5/6]

Every GTA game has one character who never dies. The city. GTA 6 is set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional take on Florida.

Frank Miller built his own city. Sin City started in April 1991, in an anthology called Dark Horse Presents. Every story happens in Basin City, and the first one follows Marv, an ex-convict, in The Hard Goodbye.

No powers. Just desperate people in a town that chews them up. Black and white, almost no gray. If you've seen the Robert Rodriguez movie, you already know the look.

[CARD: Sin City: The Hard Goodbye, Frank Miller. Mature readers.]

[YOUR VERDICT if you read it.]

Five slots filled. Planner. Thief. Driver. Heat. City. One left. The one GTA 6 is actually about.

### Slot 6: The Couple, Violent Love

[CREW 5/6, sixth slot flashing]

Jason Duval grew up around small-time crooks. After a stint in the Army, he ended up in the Keys, working for local drug runners.

Lucia Caminos just got out of Leonida Penitentiary. Her father taught her to fight, and defending her family is what put her inside.

Rockstar says a life with Jason could be her way out. People keep comparing them to Bonnie and Clyde.

In 2016, Image Comics put out a crime romance in that mold. It's called Violent Love, by writer Frank J. Barbiere and artist Victor Santos.

Daisy Jane and Rock Bradley are two of the most notorious bank robbers in the American Southwest. Then they fall in love, and the love story becomes a crime spree.

No powers. Just two people who make each other more dangerous.

And look at how it's built. The first pages show you it won't end well for them. You know where it lands. The book makes you need to know how.

That's the exact question GTA 6 is about to keep you up at night with. How does it end for Jason and Lucia? Violent Love answers that for its own couple up front, and that's why you won't put it down.

[CARD: Violent Love Vol. 1: Stay Dangerous.]
