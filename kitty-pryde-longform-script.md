## Script

**0:00** The Sentinels in the Doomsday trailer come from a comic where a 13-year-old girl was the hero. The 2014 movie gave her part to Wolverine.

**0:12** The story is Days of Future Past, from the Uncanny X-Men comics in 1981. Mutants are hunted by giant robots called Sentinels. One last plan: send someone back in time to stop it. In the comic, that someone is Kitty Pryde.

**0:40** The mind sent back belongs to an adult Kitty from the bad future. A telepath named Rachel Summers sends it into Kitty's younger self in 1980. That's the story.

**1:05** Quick pause. If you've seen the movies and never read a comic, I made a free X-Men Starter Kit. Five comics, in order, starting with this story. The link is in the description.

**1:25** Here's what the movie did. Kitty still matters. She's the one who sends Wolverine's mind back to 1973. But sending someone is not going. The hero who wakes up in the past is Wolverine. In the writer's words, "Kitty's not the one who goes back in time, Kitty's the one who sends someone back."

**2:15** The reasons they gave. Simon Kinberg, the writer, said the film's Kitty wasn't even born in 1973. He also said Hugh Jackman hasn't aged, which made him a natural fit, and he admitted Wolverine's popularity was a factor. He later called giving Kitty the time power "kind of a cheat" and explained it as a secondary mutation.

**3:20** Here's my read. [Your opinion in your words: is that a fair reason or a convenient one?] This is my opinion, not a fact.

**4:10** Remember who Kitty is. She debuted in Uncanny X-Men number 129 in 1980, created by Chris Claremont and John Byrne. She's 13. Her power is phasing: she walks through walls. The comic gave a kid the job of saving the future. [Your receipt: what you read, and what stuck with you.]

**5:30** In the film, Kitty is the one who sends Wolverine's mind back. She's an important part of the plan, but not the one who goes. Kitty fans have said for years that the movie took her story and gave it to Wolverine.

**6:40** Which brings us back to the trailer. The Doomsday trailer shows Sentinels again. If the movies keep using this story, the comic is where the original idea lives. And that's the point of this channel: you don't need 60 years of continuity to start comics. You need the right two issues.

**7:30** The comic is Uncanny X-Men number 141 and 142. It's short. You can read it tonight through your library app. [Your verdict: Buy / Library / Skip, in your words.] Now that you know where the Sentinels came from, watch [the Sentinels Short or your next long-form] next.
