## 2. Cue sheet (glance only)
- **Debut:** Fantastic Four #5, July 1962, Stan Lee and Jack Kirby.
- **Origin issue:** Fantastic Four Annual #2, 1964. Reed is right, the machine explodes, Doom blames Reed.
- **His mother:** Astonishing Tales #8 (annual fight with a demon); Triumph and Torment, 1989, Roger Stern and Mike Mignola (Strange wins the contest, Doom second, a favor, they go to hell).
- **Silver Surfer:** Fantastic Four #57 to 60, 1966 to 67.
- **Doomquest:** Iron Man #149 to 150, 1981; writer David Michelinie: "Tony's reflection in a dark mirror."
- **Unthinkable:** Mark Waid and Mike Wieringo, 2003. Doom sends Franklin to hell; Reed learns magic from Strange; Doom ends up in hell in Franklin's place.
- **Doomwar:** 2010. Doom invades Wakanda for its vibranium.
- **Secret Wars:** 1984 (Doom steals the Beyonder's power, loses it); 2015, Jonathan Hickman and Esad Ribic (God Emperor Doom, Battleworld, Reed heals his face).
- **Infamous Iron Man:** Brian Michael Bendis and Alex Maleev. **One World Under Doom:** 2025.
- **Movies:** Downey announced at San Diego Comic-Con, July 2024; Avengers: Doomsday opens this December.
- **Say it exactly:** "Victor von Doom, Doctor Doom, if you're nasty." It's the last line, after the next-video line.
- **Do not ad-lib:** Annual #2, Triumph and Torment and Unthinkable are things you have not read, so give no verdicts on them. Only Secret Wars gets "Buy."
- **Cut, so do not say:** Doom's "first word" for Valeria; Reed being held by Inhumans; Doom maiming Reed; the mirror scene in Annual #2; whether the ruler was a baron or a king.

## 3. Checklists

**Before you record**
- Optional: read Fantastic Four Annual #2 first, about 30 minutes (Marvel Unlimited works until Dec 7). It would let you give a verdict on it.
- Skim the cue sheet once; keep it next to the camera.
- Say your own words in the brackets' places slowly; they are the heart of the video.

**Before you post**
- Show the first 10 seconds to someone who does not read comics. If they hand the phone back, re-cut the opening.
- Check the sign-off is in.

**When you upload**
- Paste the title, description, tags and pinned comment from the package. Update the chapter timestamps after editing.
- **Tags:** make sure `vactormail@gmail.com` is not there. YouTube Studio, Settings, Upload defaults, Tags.
- **End screen:** the Nightcrawler video and subscribe, in the last 10 to 20 seconds. Add a card to Nightcrawler near the end.
- **Thumbnail:** your face plus Doom's mask. Text: "HE WAS WARNED" (alternate: "PRIDE IS THE VILLAIN").
- Pin the comment with the Starter Kit link.

**After you post**
- Reply to every comment in the first hour; heart the good ones.
- Post a community update: "You picked Doom. It's up."
- At 24 and 72 hours, ask me to pull views, average viewed (target about 40% or better), click-through, subscribers per 1K, and Starter Kit clicks.
- **The test:** under about 3K views at 72 hours means no more Doom long-forms. Send me the Studio numbers I can't see (click-through, viewed vs swiped away).
- If a fact turns out wrong, pin a correction. Don't hide it.
